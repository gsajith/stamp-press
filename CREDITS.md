- `flow1`: streamlines, Andrei Kashcha, MIT, https://github.com/anvaka/streamlines

- `quilt1`: tilework and p5ycho (patchwork sketches), Kjetil Golid, MIT, https://github.com/kgolid/tilework, https://github.com/kgolid/p5ycho
